Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in 3D Print Cost Calculator for both iOS and Android platforms.

1. We Collect No Personal Information

We do not collect, use, save, or have access to any of your personal data stored on your device. The 3D Print Cost Calculator is a utility tool designed to work entirely offline.

Any data you input into the app (such as filament costs, printer settings, or client names for invoices) is stored strictly on your local device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

2. PDF Generation & Storage

The app allows you to generate PDF invoices. This process happens locally on your device. The app does not upload these files to any external servers. You have full control over where these files are saved or shared via your device's native sharing functions.

3. Third-Party SDKs

To ensure maximum privacy, our app does not use any third-party tracking SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks that collect user data on either iOS or Android versions.

4. Compliance

Our policy aligns with the Apple Privacy Nutrition Label requirements and the Google Play Data Safety section, confirming that no user data is collected or shared.
