This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in 3D Print Cost Calculator. We believe in absolute privacy, ensuring that your business data remains exactly where it belongs: on your device.

1. We Collect No Personal Information

We do not collect, use, save, or have access to any of your personal data. The 3D Print Cost Calculator is a utility tool designed to work entirely offline. Any data you input—such as filament costs, printer settings, or client names—is stored strictly on your local device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

2. PDF Generation & Storage

All PDF invoices are generated locally. The app does not upload these files to external servers. You maintain full control over your documents via your device’s native sharing functions (AirDrop, Email, or Local Storage).

3. Third-Party SDKs

To ensure a clean and secure experience, our app does not use third-party tracking SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks. Your behavior within the app is not monitored.

4. Compliance

Our policy is fully compliant with the Apple Privacy Nutrition Label and Google Play Data Safety requirements. We confirm that zero user data is collected, shared, or sold.
